# Red Flags Reference — Accounting & Earnings-Quality Warning Signs

Use this during **Layer 2 (Fundamentals)** and **Layer 8 (Risk)**. None of these is a verdict on its own — they are signals that warrant a closer look. Weight of evidence matters: one flag is a question, several together is a thesis-killer.

---

## Cash-vs-Earnings Divergence (the big one)

| Signal | What it looks like | Why it matters |
|--------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Net income up, FCF flat/down** | Reported profit grows but cash generation doesn't follow | Earnings may be driven by accruals, not cash |
| **Rising accruals** | Accrual ratio = (Net Income − FCF) / Avg Total Assets, trending positive | Profit is increasingly "on paper" |
| **CFO < Net Income for multiple years** | Operating cash flow persistently below reported earnings | Classic earnings-quality warning |
| **Negative FCF with positive net income** | GAAP profit but the business burns cash | Check whether it's growth capex (ok) or working-capital bleed (not ok) |

## Revenue-Recognition & Receivables

| Signal | What it looks like | Why it matters |
|--------|-------------------|----------------|
| **DSO rising faster than revenue** | Days Sales Outstanding climbing | Sales pulled forward or channel stuffing; collections weakening |
| **Deferred revenue shrinking** | For subscription/SaaS, deferred rev declining while revenue "grows" | Future bookings are drying up |
| **Growing gap: bookings vs. billings vs. revenue** | Revenue recognized ahead of cash | Aggressive rev-rec |
| **Large "other" or one-time revenue lines** | Recurring reliance on non-core, non-repeatable revenue | Headline growth isn't organic |

## Inventory & Margins

| Signal | What it looks like | Why it matters |
|--------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Inventory rising faster than sales** | Inventory/COGS growing, turns slowing | Demand softening; write-down risk ahead |
| **Gross margin suddenly expanding without explanation** | Margin jump not tied to mix/pricing | Possible cost capitalization or channel timing |
| **Capitalizing costs that peers expense** | R&D/software costs on the balance sheet vs. income statement | Inflates current earnings, defers the hit |

## Balance-Sheet & Leverage

| Signal | Why it matters |
|--------|----------------|
| **Rising debt while buying back stock** | Financial-engineering EPS growth; fragile if rates rise or cash flow dips |
| **Interest coverage falling below ~3x** | Thin cushion; a downturn threatens solvency |
| **Debt/EBITDA climbing above sector norm** | Leverage risk (context matters — utilities/REITs run higher) |
| **Goodwill a large % of assets** | Acquisition-driven; impairment risk if deals underperform |
| **Negative book equity from buybacks** | Makes ROE meaningless — switch to ROIC/ROA |
| **Off-balance-sheet or heavy operating leases** | Understated true leverage |

---

## Share Count & Dilution

| Signal | Why it matters |
|--------|----------------|
| **Share count rising ~despite buybacks~** | Stock-based comp dilution exceeds repurchases; buybacks are just mopping up SBC |
| **Non-GAAP excludes large recurring SBC** | "Adjusted" earnings flatter reality; always check GAAP too |
| **Frequent share issuance to fund operations** | Serial dilution; existing holders bleed ownership |

---

## Governance & Behavioral

| Signal | Why it matters |
|--------|----------------|
| **Cluster of insider selling with no 10b5-1 plan** | Those closest to the business are cashing out at market |
| **CFO or auditor departure** | Highest-signal red flag; ask why, especially near a reporting period |
| **Frequent restatements or "one-time" charges every quarter** | "One-time" that recurs isn't one-time |
| **Aggressive, promotional management tone** | Guidance consistently framed to the most flattering metric |
| **Related-party transactions** | Potential self-dealing; scrutinize the footnotes |
| **Auditor changes / qualified opinions** | Independent check is flagging something |

---

## Quick Screen (run these four first)

1. **CFO vs. Net Income** over 3 years — does cash track profit?
2. **FCF trend** — growing, flat, or negative?
3. **Diluted share count trend** — shrinking (good) or creeping up (dilution)?
4. **DSO + inventory turns** — stable, or deteriorating faster than revenue grows?

If all four are clean, earnings quality is probably fine. If two or more flash, dig into the 10-K footnotes before trusting the headline numbers.

> Reminder: red flags describe *risk*, not certainty of fraud. Many are explainable (a heavy-capex growth year, a large but disclosed acquisition). The job is to find them, ask why, and price the risk — not to auto-reject.
